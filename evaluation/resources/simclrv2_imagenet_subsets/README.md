These are unmodified copies of the ImageNet 1% and 10% image-name lists
published by SimCLRv2 and used by iBOT for frozen-feature semi-supervised
evaluation:

https://github.com/google-research/simclr/tree/master/imagenet_subsets

The evaluator resolves every listed basename against the normal ImageNet-1K
training directory. `1percent.txt` has 12,811 names and SHA-256
`71e82a48ba78252683ae334c4d019ebd5c6e855b9599c1389fbe83eb9549cf17`.
`10percent.txt` has 128,116 names and SHA-256
`6d09de11e7bdaf5b1f3b1f249b6183695f97310cdd20f0c03e7235b6b9392091`.
